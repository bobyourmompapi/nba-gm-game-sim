# NBA Legends GM Game

A lightweight, browser-based NBA General Manager simulator featuring historic legends.

## How to Play
1. Open `index.html` in any web browser.
2. Enter your city name to start your career.
3. **Free Agents**: Use your $100M budget to sign legendary players like Michael Jordan, LeBron James, and Kobe Bryant.
4. **Roster**: Manage your team. You can release players to recoup their salary if you need budget for someone else.
5. **Schedule**: Simulate games against AI opponents. Your chances of winning depend on your team's average rating. You need at least 5 players to simulate a game.

## Features
- **Historic Roster**: High-fidelity ratings for NBA legends.
- **Budget Management**: Strategic signing with a hard salary cap.
- **Game Simulation**: Simple logic based on team vs. opponent ratings.
- **Log System**: Track your season's progress in real-time.

## Files
- `index.html`: The main structure and UI.
- `style.css`: The visual design and layout.
- `game.js`: The game engine, logic, and state management.
